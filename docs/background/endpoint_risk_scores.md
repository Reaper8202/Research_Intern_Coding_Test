# Clinical risk scores for the 40 target conditions

Reviewed on 28 October 2026. Updated on 29 October 2026 after the intended baseline comparison was clarified. The endpoint list is `research_intern_coding_test/target_conditions.csv`. It contains 40 rows and 40 unique codes. The task predicts the first recorded diagnosis within five years after the anchor date.

**For the proposed forecasting baseline, start with four endpoint candidates: coronary heart disease, myocardial infarction, stroke, and atrial fibrillation. Heart failure is a conditional fifth candidate.** The wider count below includes tools that do not fit this comparison. Input coverage and validation outcomes further limit the shortlist. See [the baseline feasibility review](clinical_reference_feasibility.md).

The conservative count is **14 of 40 conditions (35%) with clear links to established clinical risk, screening, or prognosis tools**. Seven have the closest links to long-term event prediction. Seven have tools for diagnosis, recovery, mortality, or a restricted clinical setting. These are counts of endpoint labels, not counts of independent scores.

Six more endpoints have partial matches to scores for a broader disease or a subset of the endpoint. Two have related imaging decision rules. For the remaining 18, this review did not confirm a qualifying score. This is a targeted review, not a systematic review of every published model. “Not confirmed” does not mean that no research model exists.

The classification uses clinical guidelines, professional society tools, and established clinical services as evidence. It does not establish how often clinicians use each tool. In particular, CHARGE-AF is a validated model recognized in the atrial fibrillation guideline. That guideline says the benefit of screening people selected by an incident-AF risk score remains unproven.

The seven category A endpoints are heart failure, stroke, myocardial infarction, coronary heart disease, osteoporotic fracture, atrial fibrillation, and prostate cancer metastasis. They are candidates for comparison, not seven ready-made five-year probabilities. Myocardial infarction is often one component of a combined cardiovascular outcome. FRAX predicts fractures at specified sites. CAPRA requires an existing prostate cancer diagnosis.

For this project, compare category A models only in eligible patients and with pre-anchor inputs. Check each model's event definition and prediction period. A 10-year probability cannot be treated as a five-year probability without a justified method. Keep category C results separate as comparisons with related outcomes.
