## 2. Findings worth fixing

Ranked. No blocker was found inside the assigned criteria.

**Should fix**

1. **D12: position encoding and output shape are untested.** Add to `tests/test_model.py` (i) `rotary` at position 0 is the identity and attention scores depend only on the position difference, (ii) swapping two event tokens with equal ages changes the last hidden state, (iii) `model(...).shape == (batch, n_targets)` with `n_targets=40` from `targets.csv`. `mechanics.py` in this directory already contains the checks.
2. **B8: make the val record complete and plain.** State in `docs/report.md` (Evaluation protocol) that official val was read three times in kind: the step-2 baselines (28-29 October, `reports/baseline/`), bundle attempt 1 (transformer ensemble), bundle attempt 2 (blend); and that round 3, which added LightGBM to the submission, was opened after attempt 1 had shown LightGBM ahead of the transformer on val mAP. The rules of round 3 were CV-only, but the choice of *what* to test was made after a val read; say so, and treat attempt 2 as a second look at the same 365 patients rather than an independent check.
3. **B9/D9: name the two mismatches behind the Platt map.** The map is fitted on OOF inputs whose epoch was chosen on the same patients (optimism D36 puts at about 0.003) and on a 3-model OOF mean plus LightGBM trained on 80 % of train, then applied to a 15-model mean plus LightGBM trained on 100 %. A one-paragraph note in D38 or the report, or a check that the test-time blend's logit distribution matches the OOF one, would close this.
4. **D10: the scratch-versus-pretrain comparison predates the submitted recipe.** Either run method S for `small-v2-c1` on the same folds (one config file, five runs) or say in `report.md:145-149` that the S/H/F comparison was made on `small-v1`/`small-v1-mm` only.
5. **D6: record the code defaults.** Fine-tuning AdamW betas (0.9, 0.999), the constant fine-tuning learning rate, and the early-stopping metric are fixed in code; list them in the Hyperparameters paragraph or move betas into `[finetune]`.

**Nits**

6. **D3:** report the held-out token-loss fraction (24.6 % on fold 0) beside the fit-set 15-18 %.
7. **D7:** say that the submitted members trained on MPS and the resume equality was established on CPU.
8. **D4:** the checkpoint state dict sums to 739,915 entries because the `output_mask` buffer is saved; anyone counting from `best.pt` gets a different number from the documented 737,984. Mention it or exclude buffers in `hf_package verify`.
9. **D11:** the report could state outright that the modalities are kept in the final model despite the null CV result and why (the report says "included at my request"; make the consequence explicit).

## 3. Appendix: outside the criteria (not scored)

* **Component rule of round 2 (C4 territory).** `configs/budget_v2.toml:107-118`: the component test (gain above 0, 4 of 5 folds) was written at 22:05 on 2 November after the A3 and X1 scores were known (quoted in the 19:29 entry), and it is laxer than the section-5.3 rule those components failed. `docs/decisions.md:384-388` discloses exactly this. The C1-versus-A1 rule itself was pre-registered and C1 passed it (+0.0083, 5/5, mAP +0.0101).
* **Pooled "calibrated" OOF numbers.** `reports/calibration/stack/calibration.json`: pooled AUROC 0.7744 raw against 0.7671 calibrated and mAP 0.2124 against 0.2056, while per-fold AUROC is identical. This is the expected artifact of five different per-fold maps (D38 explains it). Any reader quoting the pooled calibrated row would under-state the blend; prefer mean-fold numbers for calibrated results.
* **Step-2 val label reader.** `src/ehrforecast/train_baseline.py:102` reads `labels.parquet` without a split filter (B10 asks for one audited val reader; the model path has one, `val_score.py:253-260`, but the step-2 module is a second).
* **Early-stopping optimism in OOF.** Every CV comparison in the register uses OOF predictions from checkpoints selected on the compared patients; D36 states the effect (+0.003). Nested epoch selection would remove it at five times the cost.
* **Presence tokens.** `[DNA]` is emitted for any patient with a genotype file (`tokenizer.py:314-315`) even when no genotype token is known; the repository's own audit found presence correlated with outcomes (B12, `docs/report.md:80-82`). With 2 test patients carrying genotypes the effect on test is bounded.
* **Pretraining never stopped early.** All 15 parents reached epoch 30 with the best held-out loss at epoch 29 (`dump_models.py`); the 60-epoch test (P1) did not help (-0.0037 AUROC). The held-out loss on target-start tokens (`held_target_start_loss`, about 1.9-2.0 against 1.45 overall on fold 0) was still the weakest part of the pretraining objective; this motivated the 5x target weight.
* **Final LightGBM feature statistics.** `lightgbm_final.py:178-179` computes `latest_values`/`all_values` over train+val+test events, but `value_features` (`lightgbm_cv.py:141-164`) and `fit_value_spec` (`lightgbm_final.py:72-82`) take carriers and medians from the training cohort only, and the spec is checked row by row against the E11 matrices (`lightgbm_final.py:240-242`). No val or test statistic reaches a fitted object (B5 territory; verified in passing).
* **Report figure versus my recount.** `docs/report.md:183-184` says val would have chosen a later epoch in 13 of 15 members for +0.008 AUROC; recomputed from `reports/val/val_curves.csv`: 13 of 15, +0.0077.
